Design a complete iOS 26 mobile flashcard app called "No Pressure Flashcards" with 16 screens in LIGHT THEME using modern iOS design with photo cards and glassmorphism.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
VISUAL DESIGN SYSTEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COLORS:
- Background: Clean white #FFFFFF and light gray #F8F9FA
- Primary accent: Bright blue #0A84FF (iOS system blue)
- Secondary accents: Soft purple #BF5AF2, Coral #FF6B6B, Green #34C759
- Text primary: Dark gray #1C1C1E
- Text secondary: Medium gray #8E8E93
- Text tertiary: Light gray #C7C7CC

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Title Large: SF Pro Display, 34pt Bold, #1C1C1E
- Title 1: SF Pro Display, 28pt Bold, #1C1C1E
- Title 2: SF Pro Display, 22pt Bold, #1C1C1E
- Headline: SF Pro Display, 17pt Semibold, #1C1C1E
- Body: SF Pro Text, 17pt Regular, #1C1C1E
- Subheadline: SF Pro Text, 15pt Regular, #8E8E93
- Caption: SF Pro Text, 12pt Regular, #C7C7CC

PHOTO CARD STYLE:
- Large photo backgrounds with rounded corners (16-24px)
- High quality lifestyle photography: mountains, coffee shops, study scenes, nature, landmarks, travel
- Warm, inviting color palette in photos (golden hour lighting, natural tones)
- Professional photography look (sharp, well-composed, aspirational)
- Photos used as card backgrounds with text overlays

GLASS OVERLAY EFFECT:
- White frosted overlay: 60-80% opacity on photo cards
- Subtle drop shadow: rgba(0,0,0,0.08), 12px blur, 4px offset
- Text readability gradient: Dark gradient overlay (rgba(0,0,0,0.3) to transparent) on photos where text appears
- Border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.05) for card separation

SHADOWS & DEPTH:
- Card shadows: 0px 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)
- Button shadows: 0px 4px 12px rgba(10,132,255,0.25) for primary buttons
- Elevated elements: 0px 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.12)
